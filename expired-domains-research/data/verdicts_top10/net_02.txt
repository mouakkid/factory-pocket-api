DOMAIN: librariesvictoria.net
IDENTITY: Victoria's Virtual Library (VVL) — the public library portal for the State of Victoria, Australia. Confirmed verbatim from the site's own /about/ page, pulled out of Common Crawl's 2013 WARC store: "Victoria's Virtual Library (VVL) is the public library portal for the State of Victoria... Contact addresses on the page: brendanf@vicnet.net.au and info@libraries.vic.gov.au. Services: Ask a Librarian (24/7 email reference staffed by librarians across Victorian public libraries), Hot Topics, Reference Bookshelf, Victorian Biographies Online, Gulliver online databases, Library Locator, catalogue links, Zine, and the sector Infonet (jobs board, events calendar, organisation directory). English/Australian market. Wayback: first capture 2000-10-18, 216 captures, last 2026-01-22. VICNET, a State Library of Victoria business unit, was shut down in 2014 — which matches the site's decay.
LINK_SOURCES: The original neighbourhood was real public-sector authority — the page links out to slv.vic.gov.au, libraries.vic.gov.au, openroad.vic.gov.au, mmv.vic.gov.au, pictures.libraries.vic.gov.au, mylanguage.gov.au, civ.org.au — but that era is gone. What Majestic can still see is 28 refdomains for 46 backlinks, 1 EDU, 0 GOV, 0 homepage links. The TF 31 / CF 10 ratio rests on a handful of surviving library "useful links" pages, i.e. link-rot residue, not living citation.
SPAM_HISTORY: Yes, confirmed and serious. Common Crawl's 2019-51 index shows the domain rebuilt as a WordPress affiliate blog: monthly archives 2018/02 through 2018/08; categories /category/coffee-makers/, /category/automotive/, /category/fashion/, /category/tools-and-equipments/, /category/tech/, /category/psychology/; posts /advantages-private-jet-charter/, /advantages-satellite-television/, /beginners-guide-producing-prints-artwork/, /benefits-investment-retirement/, /buy-best-folding-wagon/, /cat-tree-furniture/; and decisively /becoming-online-gambling-agent/. Authors are randomised handles /author/hubertjhgf54/ and /author/judiasw23e4/. So an expired-domain buyer already harvested this name, ran high-CPC affiliate plus online-gambling content on it, and kept it answering 200 until January 2026.
TRADEMARK_RISK: high — the name maps onto an Australian public-sector library service. It was funded by Multimedia Victoria and operated by VICNET/State Library of Victoria, and "Libraries Victoria" is today the live brand of the Libraries Victoria Consortium (25 public library services, libsvic.org.au, plus a "Libraries Victoria" app on Google Play), with Public Libraries Victoria at publiclibrariesvictoria.net.au. Any commercial use reads as impersonating a government library service and hands the consortium a clean complaint.
VERDICT: REJET
JUSTIFICATION: The provenance is the best in this batch — a state-government-funded public library portal running from 2000 — but the equity has already been strip-mined by someone else: an affiliate/PBN rebuild that carried an online-gambling-agent page and ran for roughly eight years is precisely the history Google discounts, and it is on the record in Common Crawl. What is left underneath is 28 refdomains and 46 backlinks, too thin to matter even if the history were clean.
ANGLE: none. The only non-conflicting use — a Victorian library directory — has no revenue model and competes with the consortium's own libsvic.org.au.
