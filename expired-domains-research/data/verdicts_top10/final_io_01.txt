LINK_QUALITY: Directory filler. The entire discoverable footprint is startup-database entries that any founder gets for free by submitting a form: VentureRadar, Tracxn, MAGNiTT, e27, CB Insights, PitchBook, Crunchbase, StartupCentrum, plus AI-tool review-farm pages (firstsales.io "brand review"). homepageRD=3 out of 48 refdomains means ~94% of referring domains point at inner pages — the deep-link pattern of aggregator/profile scrapers, not editorial citation. The 35 registered TLDs are the signature of active brands defending the string, not of latent value. (USPTO's search API was unreachable from this environment, so this is live-commercial-use evidence, not a register search.)
VERDICT: REJET
JUSTIFICATION: Paying $35-70/yr means renting a name that a live, currently-rebranding US company (Autoly / PRP-NE) is actively building on, while the residual "authority" is a pile of startup-directory profiles pointing at a dead Canadian car app. The automotive meaning also fights the .io TLD's dev/SaaS audience, so there is no end-user for this as a brand. Any development or resale play here invites a cease-and-desist from a party with a stronger, in-use claim.
ANGLE: none
