DOMAIN: twoslash.ai
PRODUCT: "TwoSlash — An OpenAI ChatGPT Chrome Extension". A GPT wrapper that injected ChatGPT into any browser text box via a "//ai|" trigger (write/rewrite/translate/grammar). Solo indie build; Chrome Web Store developer listed simply as "twoslash", contact 2000hrithik@gmail.com. Launched 2023 (archive_first=2023), abandoned.
TRACTION: Effectively zero. Chrome Web Store listing (id nejdeohbihmiaamplfbgdoeaoikcanjm) shows **128 users, 2 ratings, last updated 18 Sep 2023** — never touched again. Zero Hacker News mentions of twoslash.ai (HN Algolia: 0 hits; the 38 "twoslash" HN hits are all the unrelated open-source TypeScript project). No funding, no Crunchbase, no press. Pricing was $9/mo or $49 BYOK — no evidence anyone paid.
LINK_QUALITY: AI-directory filler, and an unusually blatant case. 5187 backlinks from only 139 refdomains = ~37 links/domain, the signature of sitewide nav/footer links from scraped tool directories. Confirmed sources: foundr.ai, slashdot.org/software/p/TwoSlash, omr.com/reviews/product/twoslash, techlaugh.com, aiknowzone.com, chantgpt.app/review/twoslash. Topic classification is 99.12% "Computers/Software/Shareware" — i.e. software-listing sites. Every one of these links is obtainable for free in an afternoon by submitting a new tool. Not an asset. TF=23/CF=17 is directory-grade, not editorial.
NAME_QUALITY: Mediocre, and worse than it first looks. "Twoslash" is 9 letters, 2 syllables, easy to say and spell — but it is semantically empty outside of dev culture (it means "//"), has zero AI or category fit, and, decisively, **the name is already occupied in developer mindshare by the well-known open-source Twoslash project** (github.com/twoslashes/twoslash, npm `twoslash`, used by typescriptlang.org/dev/twoslash and Shiki; 26-point HN front-page item Jan 2024). Anyone Googling "twoslash" lands on the OSS tool, not on you. A brand you have to fight an incumbent meaning for is not a short-name win.
TRADEMARK_RISK: medium. No registered mark found, but the OSS Twoslash project has clear, documented, continuous prior use (originating with Orta Therox on the Microsoft TypeScript team) and an active community. No UDRP exposure for merely holding it, but commercial use in the developer-tools space would be confusingly similar, and the OSS side is exactly the constituency that makes noise.
VERDICT: REJET
JUSTIFICATION: A 128-user abandoned Chrome extension whose entire "link equity" is replicable AI-directory sitewides is worth nothing, and the name is pre-empted by a better-known open-source project of the same name — so even the name-only play is compromised. There is no plausible buyer at $70–200 who is not the OSS project, and OSS projects do not buy domains. Paying the two-year .ai carrying cost here is a straight loss.
ANGLE: none.
